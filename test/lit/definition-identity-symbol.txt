# The IR names a definition by its DSDL identity and a function by that identity with dotted
# suffixes; each backend spells them its own way. A symbol spelt for C folded `.` into `_`, so
# `ns.A_B` and `ns.A.B` were one symbol and every target failed with "redefinition of symbol", and
# their C include guards and version sentinels were one macro.
