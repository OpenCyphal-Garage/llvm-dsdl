# Where a consumer cannot reach a type from its name and the namespace, the naming manifest reports
# the name qualified by every scope that encloses it, as the language qualifies a name. C++ declares a
# service's sections in the service's struct, a type declared apart is published under its alias, and
# a namespace may be escaped; each qualified name the manifest reports names a type the generated
# headers declare, in every profile.

# RUN: printf '@deprecated\nuint8 a\n@sealed\n' > %t/ns/Old.1.0.dsdl
# RUN: printf '@deprecated\nuint8 a\n@sealed\n---\nuint8 b\n@sealed\n' > %t/ns/Read.1.0.dsdl
