# A scalar at a constant byte-aligned offset, of a width the target has a register for, is one
# load or one store on a little-endian target: the wire is little-endian, so a plain access is its
# own encoding there. The buffer's size is checked first, and short of the field the runtime's
# primitive answers as it always has, zero-extending what is there. Without the target's word the
# lowering keeps the primitive everywhere.
